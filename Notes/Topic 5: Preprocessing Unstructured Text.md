### Lesson 2: Regular Expressions - Metacharacters

* "[ ]" used to specify special characters
  - ex: [abc]=[a-c] and will match a, b, c
  - ex: [a-z] matches lowercase letters
* ^ gets complimentary set
  - [^5] will match any character except 5
